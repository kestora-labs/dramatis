# Ada Mbeki

Relay chief at Kanto Station. Eleven years on the same rotation, three of them alone.

Ada is the corpus's centre. She is competent past the point of being liked for it, and she
has arranged her life so that competence is the only thing anyone can ask of her.

## Relationships

### Tomas Reiner — brother, estranged

They have not spoken since the Berthold decision. Ada considers the estrangement settled and
Tomas does not, which is the entire difficulty. Neither has told anyone at Kanto that they
are related.

*Status: settled. This is load-bearing and should not be softened.*

### The Quorum — employer, adversary

Ada reports to the Quorum and does not trust it. She has never said so aloud on an open
channel, which is itself a fact about her.

*Status: settled.*

### Berthold — mentioned only

Named in the Berthold decision. Whether Berthold is a person, a station, or a ruling is
deliberately unresolved.

*Status: open.*
