# Cast — *The Lamplighter's Daughter*

Working notes. Not narrative; this is the reference document of the fixture.

## Auber Vance

The lamplighter. Forty years on the same round. Widowed. Believes the borough owes him
something it has never named.

- **Neve** — his daughter. He is proud of her and cannot say so.
- **Idris Kell** — the inspector who cut his round in half. Auber blames him personally.

## Neve Vance

Nineteen. Keeps the accounts at Ferrin's chandlery. Sees more of the borough's ledgers than
her father sees of its streets.

- **Auber Vance** — her father. She manages him.
- **Idris Kell** — she finds him reasonable, which she has not told her father.

## Idris Kell

Borough inspector. Efficient, unimaginative, and not unkind. Genuinely does not understand
that the round he cut was a life.

- **Auber Vance** — a file, until he is not.

## Mrs Ferrin

Owns the chandlery. Employs Neve. Appears in chapter 2 only, and is here to test that a
minor character with one scene is not inflated into a principal one.
