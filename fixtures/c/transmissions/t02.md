# Transmission Two — The Audit Notice

The Quorum's notice arrived on the open channel, which meant everyone at Kanto read it
before Ada did.

> AUDIT OF RELAY OPERATIONS, KANTO. AUDITOR ASSIGNED. COOPERATION IS ASSUMED.

No name. The Quorum never gave names in notices; it gave assurances instead, which was
worse.

Yeong found her on the gantry an hour later.

"You've read it."

"I've read it," Ada said.

"You're not surprised."

"I'm not surprised." Ada watched the low band scroll past on the handheld, saying nothing.
"Sister, if the auditor asks you about the carrier — "

"I logged it as weather."

"I know what you logged it as. I'm asking what you'll say."

Yeong considered this for a while. "I'll say I logged it as weather," she said, "and I'll
let him decide whether to ask a second question."

Ada laughed once, without much in it. "That's the most useful thing anyone's said to me
this year."
