# Chapter Two

Mrs Ferrin kept her ledgers the way she kept her shop, which was to say badly and with
enormous confidence. Neve had spent two years quietly correcting both.

"The borough's cut the lamp round," Mrs Ferrin said, not looking up. "Half of it. They're
putting the bridge end on the gas."

Neve's pen did not stop. "Since when?"

"Since Thursday, I should think. It's in the notices." Mrs Ferrin turned a page. "Your
father won't like it."

"No."

"Will he take the half?"

"He'll take it," Neve said, "and he'll hate the man who gave it to him, and he'll hate him
by name, because that's easier than hating the borough." She blotted the column and closed
the book. "What's the man's name?"

Mrs Ferrin found the notice. "Kell. Inspector Idris Kell."

Neve wrote it down, which surprised her. She had not meant to.
