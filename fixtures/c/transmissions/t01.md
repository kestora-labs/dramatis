# Transmission One — Carrier

The relay had been down eleven minutes before Ada admitted it was not weather.

"Sister, are you reading a carrier on the low band?"

Yeong's voice came back thin. "I'm reading something. I wouldn't call it a carrier."

"Call it what you like. Is it there?"

"It's there." A pause, the particular pause Yeong used when she was deciding how much to
say. "Ada. It's addressed."

Ada put both hands flat on the desk. In eleven years at Kanto nothing had been addressed.
Traffic came through, and traffic went out, and none of it had ever known her name.

"To the station," she said. "Addressed to the station."

"To you."

Ada did not answer for long enough that Yeong said her name twice.

"Log it as weather," Ada said at last.

"That isn't what it is."

"Log it as weather, Sister. I'll tell you why when I know why."
