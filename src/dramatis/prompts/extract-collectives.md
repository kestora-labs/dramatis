This work is studied with collectives counted as actors, so the instruction above not to report a group is replaced by what follows.

Report a group as a character when the passage treats it as one that acts, speaks, or is acted upon: a faction, a house, a crew, an order, a family moving as a body. Give it the kind `collective`. Report the named members too, as their own characters — a group never stands in place of the people in it.

Do not report a group that is only a way of referring to people already named. "His two sisters" in a passage that names both sisters is those two characters, not a third. The test is whether the passage treats the group as doing something the members are not separately shown doing.
