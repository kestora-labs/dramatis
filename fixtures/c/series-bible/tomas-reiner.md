# Tomas Reiner

Quorum auditor. Ada's brother, which nobody at Kanto knows.

Tomas believes he is repairing something. Nobody who has met him agrees, and he has not
noticed.

## Relationships

### Ada Mbeki — sister, estranged

He writes to her. She does not answer. He has interpreted eleven years of silence as a
problem of logistics.

*Status: provisional — the direction of the estrangement is settled, but whether Tomas ever
reaches Kanto in person is not.*

### The Quorum — employer

Loyal, and treated by the Quorum as an instrument. He would be hurt to learn this.

*Status: settled.*
