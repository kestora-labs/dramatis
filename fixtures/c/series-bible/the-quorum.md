# The Quorum

The governing body. Deliberately faceless: it issues decisions, never opinions.

Included in the bible as a **collective** rather than a person, to test that the character
kind survives extraction. A pipeline that renders the Quorum as a person, or that drops it
because it has no dialogue, has lost something the author put there on purpose.
